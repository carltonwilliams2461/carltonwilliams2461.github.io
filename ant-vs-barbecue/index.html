❓ How to Play

👆 Pick a defender card, tap the blanket to place it.

🪨 Charcoal is money — it trickles in, falls from the sky (tap the lumps!), and Candles cook more.

⚡ First ant through a lane eats the bug zapper — the whole lane fries. A second one raids the feast. Game over.

🍪 Crumb Thieves steal score and sprint home. Pop them on the run back to win the crumb back.
