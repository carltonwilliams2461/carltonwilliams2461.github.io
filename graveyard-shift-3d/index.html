How to Play

🪦 Zombies shuffle from the graves (left) toward the Gate (right). If the Gate's HP hits 0, the night restarts — you keep everything you've built.

🏮 Tap a stone plot to build: Lanterns zap, Sprinklers splash, Bells toll and slow, and the Purifier Chalice channels a zombie into a golden Mill worker instead of killing it.

🦴 Bones pay for everything. Workers earn bones forever — even offline (up to 8 hours).

👆 Bonk zombies by tapping them. ⬆️ The upgrade tree is permanent. 🌟 Every 5 nights held unlocks a New Moon prestige: reset the graveyard, keep a Moon Shard — +20% bones, +15% damage, forever.

👹 On every 5th night, The Undertaker walks. Be ready.
