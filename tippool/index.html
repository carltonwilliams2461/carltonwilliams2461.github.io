How to read this

Effective hourly rate is total per shift ÷ hours. It is the number to compare when you are choosing between a higher-wage slow room and a lower-wage busy one.

Worked check: $7.25 × 6 hours = $43.50 wages. $180 tips minus 15% tip-out ($27) leaves $153 net tips. Total $196.50, which is $32.75 per hour effective.

THE METHOD

Fair pooling is arithmetic, not arguments

A pool split by hours alone punishes the closer who worked the rush. A split by points alone ignores who was actually there. Hours × points does both jobs at once, and writing it down ends most end-of-night disputes.

  1. Agree the pool total first. Cash plus card tips for the shift, counted by two people where the house requires it.
  2. Agree points by role. Set them before the shift, not after the money is counted.
  3. Multiply hours × points. That is each person's weighted units for the night.
  4. Divide proportionally. Every share is the pool times that person's units divided by the team total.
