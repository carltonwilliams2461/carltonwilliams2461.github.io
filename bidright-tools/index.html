THE METHOD

Why “cost plus 30%” can still lose money

A 30% markup on a $10,000 cost produces an $13,000 price and only a 23.1% gross margin. A true 30% margin requires a $14,285.71 price. BidRight keeps that distinction visible and layers overhead and contingency before profit is calculated.

  1. Start with direct costs. Materials, burdened labor, subcontractors, rentals, permits, and job-specific travel.
  2. Recover overhead. Rent, software, vehicles, insurance, phones, and office time do not disappear because they are not on the job site.
  3. Add contingency deliberately. Protect the estimate from normal unknowns instead of silently eating them later.
  4. Price from margin. Gross profit is what remains after direct job costs; margin states that profit as a share of the selling price.

QUESTIONS

Built for owners, estimators, and office managers

Does BidRight store my numbers?

No. Calculations happen locally in this page. The current version has no account, database, analytics, or server-side form submission.

Can I use the quote as a contract?

No. The printable output is an estimating summary. Use your own terms, scope, exclusions, change-order process, licensing, and legal documents for a binding agreement.
