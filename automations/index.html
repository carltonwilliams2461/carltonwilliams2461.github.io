Straight answers (FAQs)

Do I need internet or a computer at my shop?

No. The system runs on our side. Your phone line just forwards calls to it — that works on a plain landline or a basic cell phone. If all you have is a phone, you have everything you need.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No — your number stays yours, and your customers keep dialing the same digits. Calls forward to the system, and you can ring your own phone first and let it catch only the calls you miss, or send every call straight to it. Your call.

What does it sound like?

Like a person answering your phone — because that's the job. It greets callers with your business name, knows your prices and hours, and books the appointment in a normal conversation. Hear it yourself in the demo above.

What if a caller asks something it doesn't know?

It doesn't guess. It takes the caller's name and number, and you get the message by text right away so you can call them back yourself.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. The setup is a one-time $500, and the service is month to month — plans run $197, $297, or $497 a month depending on the tier you pick. If it stops earning its keep, you can cancel before the next month starts.
